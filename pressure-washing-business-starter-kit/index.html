What is in the kit

  • Pricing & Profit Calculator (Excel). Five services (house wash, driveway or concrete, deck, roof soft wash, gutters) with your own price per unit, minimum charge, production rate and chemical cost. It adds labor, travel, equipment wear and an overhead share, then shows the quote, cost, profit, margin, profit per crew-hour and the minimum price for your target margin. A job log holds up to 100 finished jobs and a 12-month projection shows monthly profit and break-even jobs.
  • Rate card, quote and invoice (Excel). Enter your business details and rates once. The customer rate card (eight example services you can rename or replace) updates itself, the quote calculates discount, tax and total, and the invoice calculates amount due and a Due / Partial / Paid status.
  • Job checklists (Word and PDF). Pre-job, on-site before you start, during and after the work, and equipment and safety.
  • Sample service agreement (Word and PDF). Scope, access, plants and runoff, weather, changes and cancellation, photos and insurance. A sample only, not legal advice.
  • Customer scripts (Word and PDF). Quote follow-ups, booking, reminders, invoice, review request, weather reschedule, extra-work approval and setting stain expectations.
  • Quick Start (PDF). A short guide that maps each file to a step in the job.

A look inside (example data)

Every rate, name, address and total shown is an example, not a market rate and not pricing advice. The $866.40 quote total comes from the example job in the files (2,400 sq ft house wash, 900 sq ft driveway and 180 ft of gutters at the example rates, less a 5% discount).

How a job flows through the kit

  1. Price it in the calculator and check the margin and the minimum price for your target.
  2. Send it using the quote template, and follow up with the quote scripts if you do not hear back.
  3. Agree it by starting from the sample agreement, after a local attorney has reviewed it.
  4. Do it with the printable checklists, including the equipment and safety list.
  5. Bill it with the invoice template, then log the job to see real profit per job.
  6. Follow up with a thank-you and, only if the job went well, a review request.

Are the prices in the files real market rates?

No. Every rate, address, customer and job in the files is labeled as an example. They are there so you can see the formulas and the layout working. Replace the yellow cells with your own numbers before you send anything to a customer.

Is the service agreement legal advice?

No. It is a sample only. Contract law differs by state and country, so have a qualified attorney in your area review and adapt it before you use it with customers. Quoteloom is not a law firm.

Does it work in Excel and Google Sheets?

The spreadsheets are standard .xlsx files with ordinary formulas and no macros. They were verified in LibreOffice Calc and are designed to open in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets (File > Import). Formatting can look slightly different in each app.

How was it checked?

The calculator's math was compared with an independent calculation in 5 test scenarios, and the shipped file was recalculated again. The rate card, quote and invoice formulas were recalculated in LibreOffice and compared with an independent model, including discounts, sales tax, minimum charges, partial payments and empty lines.
