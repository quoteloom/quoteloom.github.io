Privacy

Last updated: November 26, 2026.

  • This website itself uses no analytics, no tracking scripts, no first-party cookies and no advertising.
  • The free calculator runs entirely in your browser. Numbers you type are not sent or stored anywhere.
  • The Quote Maker also runs in your browser. Your business profile, logo, quote drafts and templates are saved only in your browser's local storage on your device, never sent to us. If you activate Quote Maker Pro, your license key is sent to Gumroad to check it, and a record of that check is kept in local storage.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Purchases happen on Gumroad, which handles payment and delivery under its own privacy policy. Quoteloom receives the order details Gumroad shares with sellers (such as your email) and uses them only to deliver the product and answer support requests.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to reply.
  • Some pages may later include affiliate links. Those links will be disclosed on the affiliate disclosure page and on the page that contains the link. Clicking an affiliate link may set a cookie or similar tracker on the destination company's site so they can credit a referral. That happens on their site, not through a tracker we install here.
