How it's calculated

  • Loan amount = purchase price × (1 − down payment %). With 100% down it is $0.
  • Loan points = loan × points %. Interest during the hold = loan × annual rate × (months / 12). That interest is interest-only, not a fully amortizing mortgage payment. Principal is treated as repaid at sale.
  • Net sale proceeds = ARV − selling costs. Profit = proceeds − purchase price − closing costs − points − rehab − holding costs − interest.
  • Cash invested = down payment + closing costs + points + rehab + holding costs + interest.
  • ROI = profit / cash invested. Annualized ROI = (1 + ROI)12 / months − 1. If the hold is 0 months, cash invested is 0, or ROI is −100% or worse, annualized ROI is n/a.
  • 70% rule maximum offer = ARV × 70% − rehab. This is a rule of thumb only. It does not subtract holding costs, interest, points, purchase closing costs or selling costs, and it is not a recommendation. A deal can show a profit in this calculator and still sit above that number, or the other way around.

Not financial advice. This is an educational estimate based only on what you enter. It ignores income taxes, cost overruns beyond the rehab you type, and a sale that takes longer than you planned. Verify numbers and talk to qualified professionals before buying, renovating or selling a property.
