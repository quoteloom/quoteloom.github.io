Home Services Business OS

A Notion workspace for pressure washing, house cleaning and lawn care businesses: clients, jobs, quotes, invoices, a services and rate list, job checklists, and copy-and-adapt follow-up scripts.

A Free Notion plan is enough to use this template in a one-person workspace. It is a planning template, not field-service software, and not legal, tax, insurance, accounting or pricing advice.

What’s in the workspace

  • Clients and Jobs — people you work for, and each scheduled or finished visit.
  • Quotes, Invoices and Line Items — what you offered, what you billed, and the services on each document.
  • Services and Rates — the list you quote from, covering pressure washing, house cleaning (including frequency discounts and add-ons) and lawn care. Shipped prices are examples only.
  • Checklist Library — job steps for each trade, as a filterable library and as readable pages.
  • Follow-up Scripts — copy-and-adapt texts for quote follow-up, booking, invoice, review request and seasonal notes. Send only what is true for that job.

You also get a short Quick Start PDF. If you buy a published Notion template, you duplicate it into your workspace. If you receive a zip, you import it in Notion on desktop or web (Settings → Import, or type /zip on a page).

What this is not

It is a starting workspace, not field-service software. There is no live payment collection, no automatic SMS, and no connection to QuickBooks or a booking site. You type your own rates, your own jobs, and the messages you actually send.

If you already use the Home Services Business Kit (the Excel calculators and forms), this is a companion, not a replacement. Use a calculator to check cost and margin, then record the price you are willing to send in this workspace. You do not need the kit to use the template.
