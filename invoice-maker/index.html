Frequently asked questions

Is the Invoice Maker really free?

Yes. You can make and download as many PDF invoices as you like. Free invoices include a small "Made with Quoteloom" line at the bottom. Quote Maker Pro (US$7/month) removes that line on both quotes and invoices and lets you pick a brand color.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage on this device. Quoteloom has no server and no database, so we never see your customers or prices. Clearing your browser data removes it, and it does not sync to your other devices. Your business profile and Pro key are shared with the Quote Maker on this same device. If you activate Pro, your license key is sent to Gumroad (our payment provider) to check it.

How do I turn a quote into an invoice?

Make the quote in the Quote Maker (it is saved in this browser as you type). Open this page and click "Import from Quote Maker." Line items, customer, discount, tax and notes come across. If the quote had a deposit, that amount is filled in as already paid; you can change it if the customer paid a different amount.

Is this invoice a tax document or a contract?

No. The tool only formats the prices, dates and terms you enter. It is not tax, legal or accounting advice, and it does not file anything or collect payment. Check what your invoices need to include where you work.
