    Frequently asked questions

    Is the Quote Maker really free?

    Yes. You can make and download as many PDF quotes as you like. Free quotes include a small "Made with Quoteloom" line at the bottom and you can keep one saved template. Pro removes the line, unlocks unlimited templates and lets you pick a brand color.

    Where is my data stored?

    In your browser's local storage on this device. Quoteloom has no server and no database, so we never see your customers or prices. Clearing your browser data removes it, and it does not sync to your other devices. If you activate Pro, your license key is sent to Gumroad (our payment provider) to check it.

    Where do the calculator prices come from?

    The "start from a calculator" lines use the example rates from our free pressure washing, house cleaning and lawn care calculators. They are not market rates. Replace them with what you actually charge.

    Is the quote a contract?

    The tool only formats the prices and terms you enter. It is not legal, tax or accounting advice; check what your quotes need to include where you work.
