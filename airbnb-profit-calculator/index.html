How it's calculated

  • Occupied nights = 365 × occupancy. Stays = occupied nights / average length of stay (0 if the stay length is 0).
  • Lodging revenue = occupied nights × nightly rate. Cleaning-fee revenue = stays × guest cleaning fee. Gross booking = lodging + cleaning-fee revenue.
  • Platform fees = gross booking × the platform percentage. Lodging tax remitted = lodging revenue × the lodging-tax percentage. Net booking = gross − platform fees − lodging tax.
  • Variable contribution = net booking − (stays × (cleaning cost + supplies)) − (net booking × co-host percentage).
  • Fixed annual costs = 12 × (housing payment + utilities + other monthly costs) + annual property tax and insurance.
  • Cash flow = variable contribution − fixed annual costs. Cash-on-cash = annual cash flow / cash invested (n/a if cash invested is $0).
  • Break-even occupancy = fixed annual costs / variable contribution at 100% occupancy (same rate and fees). If that contribution is $0 or less, the result is “Not reachable.”

Frequently asked questions

How do I estimate Airbnb or short-term rental profit?

Start with the nightly rate and occupancy you actually expect, not a best-case month. Multiply occupied nights by the rate, add cleaning fees you charge guests, then subtract platform fees, lodging tax you remit, cleaning and supply costs, and your monthly housing payment, utilities and other fixed costs. The result is estimated cash flow, not a guarantee.

What occupancy should I assume?

There is no typical rate that works everywhere. This page ships with an example occupancy of 62% and a $165 average nightly rate so the math is visible. Use your own figures, and remember occupancy and rates usually change by month — the spreadsheet lets you enter all 12 months.

Does this include Airbnb or Vrbo fees and lodging tax?

Yes, as percentages you enter. The example uses 15% for platform and payment fees on gross booking (lodging plus the guest cleaning fee) and 6% lodging tax on lodging revenue only, assuming you remit it. Replace both with the rates that apply to your listing and jurisdiction.

What is on this page versus the spreadsheet?

This free calculator uses one average nightly rate and one occupancy for a 365-day year, and you type the monthly housing payment yourself. The Quoteloom spreadsheet adds month-by-month seasonality, two scenarios side by side, own-versus-lease (rental arbitrage), a calculated mortgage payment, furnishing amortization and tax or maintenance reserves.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an educational estimate that uses only the numbers you enter, plus the example figures shown on the page. It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and it is not a market survey. Platform fees, occupancy taxes and local short-term-rental rules change. Verify every number and consult qualified professionals before you buy, lease or list a property.
