Rental Host OS

A Notion workspace for short-term rental hosts and for people who also keep long-term rental properties: properties, guests and tenants, bookings, turnovers, expenses, maintenance, and a simple owner statement for a month.

A Free Notion plan is enough to use this template in a one-person workspace. It is a planning template, not property-management software, and not legal, tax, insurance, accounting or investment advice.

What’s in the workspace

  • Properties and Guests — the units you host or let, and stay guests or long-term tenants.
  • Bookings — stays and monthly rent, with nights, rates, cleaning fees, channel fees and payouts.
  • Cleanings and Turnovers — turnover visits and what you pay the cleaner, which may differ from the cleaning fee a guest paid.
  • Expenses and Maintenance — supplies, utilities, insurance, landscape and repairs, linked to a property and a monthly statement.
  • Owner Statements — a monthly income, cost and net figure for each property.
  • Checklists and messages — turnover, inspection and monthly walk-through lists, plus copy-and-adapt guest, cleaner and tenant scripts. Send only what is true.

You also get a short Quick Start PDF. If you buy a published Notion template, you duplicate it into your workspace. If you receive a zip, you import it in Notion on desktop or web (Settings → Import, or type /zip on a page).

What this is not

It is a starting workspace, not property-management software. There is no live payment collection, no connection to a booking site, and no automatic messaging. You type the figures and the messages you actually send.

If you already use Quoteloom’s short-term rental profit calculator or the Property Investor Business Kit, this is a companion, not a replacement. Use a calculator to check a deal or a month, then record what actually happened here. You do not need those products to use this template.
