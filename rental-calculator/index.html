Operating expenses = tax, insurance, HOA, management, repairs, capital reserves and owner-paid utilities per month, not the mortgage. Example values shown; use your own.

How it's calculated

  • Mortgage payment = loan × r / (1 − (1 + r)−n), with r = annual rate / 12 and n = years × 12. At 0% it is loan / n; with 100% down it is $0.
  • NOI (net operating income) = rent × (1 − vacancy) − operating expenses.
  • Cash flow = NOI − mortgage payment.
  • Cap rate = annual NOI / purchase price. Cash-on-cash = annual cash flow / (down payment + closing costs + repairs).

Not financial advice. This is an educational estimate based only on what you enter. It ignores income taxes, depreciation and selling costs. Verify numbers and talk to qualified professionals before buying property.
