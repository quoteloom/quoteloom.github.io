Vacation Rental Turnover Checklist

A starting list for the work between stays. This is not a health, safety, or legal standard.

Before you enter

  • Confirm the checkout time and that the guests have left.
  • Bring the supply tote and the linens for this unit.
  • Note any damage or missing items on arrival, with a photo.

Rooms

  • Strip beds and start the laundry, or bag linens for off-site service.
  • Remake beds with clean linens.
  • Wipe surfaces, switches and remotes.
  • Vacuum and mop floors.
  • Empty bins and replace bags.

Kitchen and bath

  • Clean kitchen surfaces, the sink, the hob and the inside of the microwave.
  • Run or empty the dishwasher; restock detergent if you provide it.
  • Clean the bathroom, including the toilet, sink, shower and mirror.
  • Restock soap, toilet paper and trash bags to your usual levels.

Before you leave

  • Reset the thermostat to your usual setting.
  • Check that lights, locks and the trash are in the usual state.
  • Take after-clean photos if that is your habit.
  • Report anything that needs a repair, with a photo, before the next arrival.

Repairs or follow-up (write in)

Do not keep door codes, lockbox codes or Wi-Fi passwords on a printed sheet that might be left behind or shared unsafely.
